# Privacy Policy for SneakerLogger

**Effective date:** August 21, 2026

SneakerLogger ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by Fernando Gonzalez ("the developer"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

## Summary

SneakerLogger stores all of your data — sneaker names, wear history, and photos — **locally on your device only**. The app has no backend server, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet access at all. We never see, collect, or receive any of your data.

## Information the App Handles

When you use SneakerLogger, you may choose to enter or provide:

- **Sneaker names** you add to your collection.
- **Wear history dates** you log for each sneaker.
- **Photos of your sneakers**, taken with your device's camera or selected from your photo library.
- **Sort preference**, saved locally so your chosen sort order persists between app sessions.

All of this information is stored exclusively in the app's private local storage on your device (a local database and local image files). It is never transmitted to the developer, to any server, or to any third party, because the app does not use the internet or any network connection.

## Permissions We Request

- **Camera** — used only when you choose to take a photo of a sneaker. The photo is saved locally; the app does not access the camera at any other time.
- **Photo library** — used only when you choose to pick an existing photo for a sneaker.

Neither permission is used for any purpose other than letting you attach a photo to a sneaker you add or edit.

## Export and Import

SneakerLogger includes an optional feature to export your entire collection (including photos) to a single file, and to import a previously exported file back into the app.

- **Export** creates a file on your device and then opens your device's native share sheet, so you can choose to save it or send it to an app of your choosing (e.g., email, cloud storage, messaging apps). Where that file goes after that point is controlled entirely by you and is subject to the privacy policy of whichever app or service you choose to share it with — the developer has no access to it.
- **Import** replaces your current collection with the contents of a file you select yourself. The app does not access or read any file unless you explicitly pick one.

## Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored in the app's private storage area on your device, which is sandboxed by the operating system (Android/iOS) so that other apps cannot access it without your permission. We do not maintain any servers or databases containing your information, because none of your information ever leaves your device unless you personally choose to export and share it.

## Data Retention and Deletion

You control your data directly within the app:

- You can delete an individual sneaker (and its wear history and photo) using the app's delete feature.
- You can replace your entire collection using the import feature.
- Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data associated with it.

## Children's Privacy

SneakerLogger does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect or transmit information at all. The app is not directed at children and contains no ads, tracking, or in-app purchases.

## Third-Party Services

SneakerLogger does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. No data is shared with third parties.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Effective date" above will be revised accordingly. Since the app does not collect contact information, we recommend checking this page periodically if you have concerns.
